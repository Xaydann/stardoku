HOW TO PLAY
Place exactly one star in every coloured region, every row, and every column.

No two stars may touch: not even diagonally.

Drag across squares to sweep X's on. Drag starting on an X wipes them off again.
Double-tap a square to place a star.

Every puzzle can be solved by pure deduction. You never need to guess.
