Stardoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Stardoku is a puzzle game. There is no account, no login, and no server of our own. This page explains the little that is collected, and by whom.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, stars, settings, streaks) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks;
  • your IP address and approximate (not precise) location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and basic device information.

If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see less personalised ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you are in the UK, EEA, Switzerland or a US state with privacy laws, you are asked for consent before any of this happens, and can change your choice later in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Stardoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Refuse tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change ad consent: in the game, Settings → Privacy choices (where it applies).
  • Remove ads entirely: the one-off "Remove ads" purchase, where offered.
  • Erase your progress: in the game, Settings → Reset everything.
